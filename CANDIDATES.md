# Candidates — Name the Robot Band

Judge brief (z-ai/glm-5.3-flash, pairwise, margin 1–5), criteria in stated
order:
1. Names a funny band made of robots + a few words on its hit song.
2. Laugh on the first read, a real joke rather than pun-shaped noise.
3. Hit-song line adds a second laugh and fits the band's name.
4. Sounds like a band that could exist, a name you'd repeat to a friend.
5. Few words, no filler, no explaining of the joke.

Field check (474 entries at build time): the field is saturated with
Roomba variants, "404 Love Not Found", "The Beep Gees", "Ctrl+Alt+Defeat",
buffering/blue-screen/firmware-crash lines, "The Unplugged" and "The Rusty
Bolts". Every candidate below avoids those shapes. A near-duplicate check
against all 474 titles found no "ATMs", "Microwaves", "Vending Machines"
entry (a "Toaster Ovens" entry exists, which also rules out the Toasters
candidate).
